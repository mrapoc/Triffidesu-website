Your school doesn't run DofE? You can still do it.

Plenty of schools don't offer the award, drop it after Bronze, or cap the places. None of that stops you. The Duke of Edinburgh's Award is run through licensed centres, and a Scout unit is one. We're one, in Ludlow.

The bit nobody tells you

School is one route to the award, not the only one

The award belongs to the Duke of Edinburgh's Award charity, not to any school. Schools deliver it as licensed centres, and so do Scout groups, cadet units, youth services and others. The certificate is identical whichever centre you go through, because it's the same award.

So if any of these is you, nothing has gone wrong and you haven't missed your chance:

  • Your school has never offered DofE
  • It offers Bronze and then stops
  • Places were limited and you didn't get one
  • You've moved school, or you're between schools
  • You're home educated
  • You started at school, and it stalled

How it works with us

Four steps, and none of them involve your school

1

You join the unit

You do the award as an Explorer, so you join Triffid first. Plenty of people have come to us for exactly that reason, with no Scouting behind them at all. You get three Mondays to see whether you like the rest of it before anyone signs anything.

2

We enrol you

We're a licensed centre, so we register you and set up your eDofE account. You can only be registered with one centre at a time, which matters if your school has already enrolled you — tell us and we'll sort out the order of things.

3

You pick your sections

Volunteering, Physical, Skills and Expedition, plus a residential at Gold. Things you already do count: a sport, an instrument, a Saturday job's worth of helping out. Activities you do at school still count too, even though you're registered with us.

4

We run the expedition

Training, practice and the assessed expedition, in the Shropshire Hills and the Brecon Beacons, with wild country for Gold. We lend the tents. There's a full kit list so nobody turns up having bought the wrong thing.

An honest comparison

Doing it outside school is often the better version anyway

It costs a fraction. Bronze through us is the £33.50 enrolment fee. Schools bundle in staff time, external providers and minibus hire, and charge £150 to £600 for the same certificate.

It runs on your timetable, not the school year. We aren't tied to an academic calendar, so you start when you're ready rather than in the September when the sign-up sheet goes up.

The expedition isn't a school trip. It's run by people who camp for fun, with a unit that camps all year, so the expedition is the thing they already do rather than an extra duty in a teacher's week.

You get the rest of it thrown in. Camps, activities, badges, and a group of people who'll still be your mates after the award is signed off.

Some school DofE programmes are excellent, and if yours is one, do it there. This page is for everyone whose school hasn't got one.
