If you're 14–18 and bored of the same old routine, we get it. Triffid Explorer Scouts is where you'll find real adventure, not sitting around.

How to join

Turn up on a Monday at 7pm. That's it.

You can try a few sessions before you decide.

1

Come along to a Monday session

No need to book, just turn up at 7pm at Ludlow Scout Hut. Drop us an email first if you'd like to let us know you're coming.

2

Try a few sessions

There's no pressure. Your young person can come along for a few weeks to see if it's for them.

3

Sign up

Once they're ready, we'll get them registered. It's quick and simple.

4

Get adventuring

That's it. They're part of the team. Camps, expeditions, badges, and a whole lot of fun await.
