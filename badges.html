How badges work here

The programme follows what you want to do

Every term the Explorers decide what we're chasing as a group. If you want a badge nobody else fancies, you can still work on it yourself and we'll sign it off.

You pick them

Every term the Explorers decide what we're chasing. Camper, Climber, Paddle Sports, Emergency Aid, they all come out of things we were doing anyway.

Or go your own way

Fancy one nobody else does? Musician, Sailing, Hobbies, Money Skills. Work on it in your own time and we'll help you plan it and sign it off.

Staged badges keep going

Nights Away, Hikes Away, Emergency Aid and others come in numbered stages. You keep climbing them for as long as you keep at it.
