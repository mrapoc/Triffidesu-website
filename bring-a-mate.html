The honest version

No PowerPoint, no “values” speech

Three things you actually care about.

01

Explorers isn't school

It's two hours on a Monday night. 7–9pm. Then home. Not a school club, not on any school radar.

02

Three Mondays free

Three sessions, no money, no sign-up, no awkward chat. Turn up, see what it is, walk away at any point.

03

You decide what we do

Every term we ask the Explorers what they want to do and we build the programme around it.

What we actually do

Real photos, real Mondays, real weekends

All from our own lot.

Paintballing

Full kit, full day, full body bruises. One of the highlights every year.

Kayaking

On the actual river with a qualified instructor. You'll get wet. That's the point.

Wide Games & Hut Nights

Games in the woods after dark, gaming on the projector at the hut, cooking challenges.
