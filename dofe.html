What is the Duke of Edinburgh Award?

The DofE is a world-renowned youth achievement award with three levels: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. It's recognised by universities and employers as evidence of commitment, resilience, and initiative.

Each level involves four sections:

  • Volunteering — giving back to your community
  • Physical — improving a sporting or fitness skill
  • Skills — developing a practical or creative skill
  • Expedition — planning and completing a journey on foot

Gold level adds a fifth section: a Residential — spending time away from home on a shared activity.

  • Ongoing support from experienced DofE leaders
  • Kit lists and advice, plus discount codes for major outdoor stores

Plus, because you're part of Explorer Scouts, you get regular outdoor experience all year round — not just a crash course before expedition day.
