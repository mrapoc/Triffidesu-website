The basics

What is the Duke of Edinburgh Award?

A world-renowned youth achievement award with three levels: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. It's recognised by universities and employers as evidence of commitment, resilience, and initiative.

Volunteering
Giving back to your community
Physical
Improving a sporting or fitness skill
Skills
A practical or creative skill
Expedition
Planning and completing a journey on foot
Gold only
A residential, away from home
What you get

How we support you

As a licensed DofE centre, we provide everything you need.

  • Full expedition training: navigation, campcraft, route planning, first aid
  • Practice and qualifying expeditions in stunning countryside
  • Help choosing and logging your volunteering, physical, and skills sections
  • Access to eDofE, the online record system
  • Ongoing support from experienced DofE leaders
  • Kit lists and advice, plus discount codes for major outdoor stores

Plus, because you're part of Explorer Scouts, you get regular outdoor experience all year round, not just a crash course before expedition day.

DofE FAQ

The questions we get asked most

Bronze is £33.50 and Silver is £33.50, which is the DofE enrolment fee. Gold is £40. On top of that there are small expedition costs for food and transport, but because we're a volunteer-run Scout unit we don't charge extra for training or leader time.
Young people can join Explorer Scouts from age 13½ and start their Bronze DofE. Unlike schools, we're not tied to an academic calendar, so you can begin when you're ready.
