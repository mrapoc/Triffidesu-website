What Explorer Scouts actually is

Explorer Scouts is the part of Scouting for young people aged 14 to 18, and you can join from 13½. It isn't a school club and it isn't Cubs. It's real outdoor adventure: you carry your own pack, cook your own food, and pick what the unit does each term.

We're a licensed Duke of Edinburgh centre, we meet every Monday from 7pm to 9pm at Ludlow Scout Hut on Station Drive, and there are 51 activity badges to go at if you want them. Subs are £15 a month once you've decided, and it's free while you're moving up from Scouts.

Where our Explorers travel in from

We're based in Ludlow, but we draw from across South Shropshire and over the Welsh and Herefordshire borders. If you're anywhere near these, you're near enough:

  • Ludlow, Bromfield and Onibury
  • Craven Arms, Clun and Bishop's Castle
  • Bucknell, Leintwardine and Wigmore
  • Knighton and Presteigne
  • Cleobury Mortimer and the Clee villages

Not sure we're your nearest unit? Find your local Scout group. We'd always say try the closest one first, but if Triffid suits you better, you're welcome here.
